# Start here

GoHook finds the Reddit conversations your buyers are already in. Then it helps you write something worth posting.

It never posts for you. You read every draft. You post it yourself.

## Why Reddit

People ask real questions there before they buy. Most brands never see those threads. Or they show up late, and sound like an ad.

## Your first ten minutes

1. Sign in with an email link or Google.
2. Run an **Audit**. It shows where you stand.
3. Use **Ask** to see what people say about you or a competitor.
4. Save the good threads to the **Board**.
5. Use **Reply to Threads** or **Ideate** to write your post.

No account yet? You get 5 searches before we ask you to sign in.

## What we will not do

We will not post for you. We will not promise results. If a number is an estimate, the screen says so.
