# Audit

**The problem:** you do not know where you stand on Reddit. You do not know where buyers ask, or who already answers them.

**What Audit does:** one score. It shows where buyers ask, where your competitors answer, and where you are missing.

## How to use it

1. Connect your Reddit account. Audit needs it. Your karma and account age tell it which communities you can post in today.
2. Enter your category. For example, "CRM for startups".
3. Add the competitors to compare with. For example, "Folk, HubSpot".
4. Run it.

## What you get

- Who is choosing what to buy without you in the room.
- Who is already talking about you.
- Which conversations to join first.

## Run it again

Audit remembers your last run. Run it again and you see if the score went up or down. You also see which threads are new.
