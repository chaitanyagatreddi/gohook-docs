# Board

**The problem:** good threads get lost in browser tabs and bookmarks.

**What the Board does:** one place for everything you want to act on. Four columns.

1. **New**
2. **Reviewing**
3. **Ready to post**
4. **Actioned**

## How to use it

1. Save a thread from Ask, Search or Graph.
2. Move the card as you work on it.
3. Drafts from Reply to Threads can go straight into Ready to post.

## What to know

It is your Reddit to-do list. Nothing on it posts by itself.
