# Ideate

**The problem:** you want to post on Reddit. You do not know what to say without sounding like an ad.

**What Ideate does:** you paste a link to your blog post, launch or changelog. Or you write your idea in two lines. GoHook finds the subreddits already talking about it. Then it gives you post ideas that start a conversation, not a pitch.

## How to use it

1. Paste a link. Or type the one thing you want readers to remember.
2. Read the three to five ideas. Each one has the subreddits to post in, and real threads that show the tone people use there.
3. Pick the conversation you want to start. Get a draft.
4. Check each subreddit before you post. Then post it yourself.

## What to know

GoHook never posts for you.

If it finds no matching conversations, it says so. Go look at each subreddit yourself before you post.
