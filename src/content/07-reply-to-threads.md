# Reply to Threads

**The problem:** writing a reply that helps, and does not sound like a sales pitch, is hard.

**What it does:** you paste a thread. You say what you want to add. GoHook writes a reply that fits the conversation.

## How to use it

1. Paste a Reddit thread link. An article link works too.
2. Say what you want to add. For example, "agree, and mention I switched to Obsidian and it loads instantly".
3. Read the draft. Edit it.
4. Save it to the Board under Ready to post. Or copy it and post it yourself.

## What to know

If you saved your voice, the draft uses it. The screen tells you when it did, and when it did not.
