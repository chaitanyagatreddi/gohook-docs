# Research

**The problem:** you need answers you can trust before you decide on pricing, products or messaging.

**What Research does:** ask up to three questions at once. The answers come only from Reddit evidence. Every claim points to a source. If the evidence is thin, it says so.

## How to use it

1. Type one to three questions.
2. Watch the checks run. The screen shows each step.
3. Read the answer. Open the sources behind each claim.
4. Ask a follow-up if you need more.

## How we check the answer

1. Work out what you are really asking.
2. Search Reddit. Drop dead links and duplicates.
3. Score how well each thread answers you.
4. If the evidence is thin, search again, wider.
5. Write the answer. Tie each claim to a source.
6. Fix or drop any source that does not hold up.

## When you get no answer

Sometimes we hold the answer back on purpose.

- A "best X" or "X vs Y" question needs three relevant threads across two communities.
- A plain "how does X work" question can pass on one strong, on-topic thread.

If neither is met, you get nothing. The screen tells you which check failed. Your credits come back.

## What it costs

Your first 10 questions are free. After that, 100 credits each. When your credits run out, Research stops until you top up.
