# Search

**The problem:** deciding which website pages to update means hours of tagging Search Console data in spreadsheets.

**What Search does:** it connects to your Google Search Console and groups your searches for you. Then it shows how many extra visitors you could get by moving each page up to page one.

## How to use it

1. Connect Search Console. GoHook only reads your data.
2. Pick your site.
3. Enter your brand name.
4. Read the ranked list. It tells you what to write or update first.
5. Select keywords. Press **Find Reddit threads**. You see where people are asking about them. If no thread matches closely enough, the screen says so.

## What to know

- The extra-click numbers are **estimates, not promises**.
- One search can sit in several groups. So the group totals will not add up to the whole.
- It looks at your top 250 searches.
