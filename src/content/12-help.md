# Help

Stuck? Write to **c@gohook.dev**.

The privacy, terms and refund pages cover the legal side.

## Common questions

**Does GoHook post for me?**
No. You read every draft. You post it yourself.

**What happens to my credits if an answer fails?**
They come back.

**Do credits expire?**
No. You use them up as you go.

**What Reddit data does GoHook read?**
Public threads, through search. It uses your connected account only where a feature needs it. Audit is one.
