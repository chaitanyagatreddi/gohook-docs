# BanAware

**The problem:** a Reddit account can be quietly limited, and you only notice when nobody replies.

**What BanAware does:** it checks whether your account's profile can be seen by someone who is not logged in. It tracks karma and age, and gives the account a score out of 100. It asks you to come back every two weeks, so a slide shows up early.

## How to use it

1. Add your Reddit username, or paste its profile link.
2. Answer the short setup. Why you want to check the account, and up to three subreddits you plan to post in.
3. Do a check-in every two weeks. It asks one question, "Did anything odd happen since your last check?", then checks the profile again.
4. Read your score and the tips that come with it.
5. Mark each subreddit green, yellow or red from its own rules. Add a short note.
6. If something goes wrong, open the "Got banned?" card. It helps you work out which kind of ban it is and what to do next.

## Alerts on the $15 plan

Connect a Slack channel in Settings, under Alerts. BanAware posts there when an account reaches Danger, when its score falls by 15 points or more, and when a check-in is due.

## What to know

The score is an estimate from what GoHook can see. It is not a promise about how Reddit treats your account.

Some checks, like your account's hidden trust grade, cannot be read automatically. They stay manual.

The bar at the top of the screen shows account health: Healthy, Fair, Danger or No Data.
